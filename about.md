# About Me

Hi, I’m Annabelle, a fourth-year Mechanical and Electrical Engineering student at The University of Edinburgh, passionate about renewable energy, sustainable systems, and the green transition.

My engineering interests focus heavily on clean technologies, specifically **solar PV integration** and **heat pumps**. I previously completed an internship with **Bellway Homes Scotland**, where I conducted a solar PV feasibility study—modelling financial payback periods, property value uplift, and green mortgage incentives, alongside evaluating retrofit options using SAP ratings and CAD drawings. 

Alongside my engineering studies, I run my own sustainable second-hand clothing business, Catharina's Closet, managing sourcing, operations, and cost tracking. I’m also an active member of the university community, participating in the **Powerlifting Society** and the **Open Water Swimming Society**.

Having served as the **Social Secretary** for the Mechanical Engineering Society last year, I currently serve as **Director of Events**, organising academic and social events for 100+ members. I am also an **Academic Parent** within the society and a passionate advocate for **women in STEM**, mentoring first-year students and supporting underrepresented groups in engineering.

My technical skills include:
- **Renewable Energy & Feasibility:** SAP ratings, solar PV financial modelling, retrofit assessment
- **Programming & Analysis:** MATLAB, Python, C++, Verilog, Excel financial modelling
- **CAD & Engineering Tools:** SolidWorks, AutoCAD, LTspice, fault-finding & diagnostics
